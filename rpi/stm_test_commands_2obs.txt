============================================================
SCENARIO
============================================================
robot start : grid (0, 0) facing NORTH
obstacle 0  : grid (18, 2), image faces WEST
obstacle 1  : grid (8, 1), image faces NORTH

============================================================
COMMANDS - type each line into PuTTY in order, top to bottom
============================================================
First: HELLO, then HB, then confirm robot is on the ground with clear space.

*** IMPORTANT: the FIRST STRAIGHT,GOCM or TURN,START command you send ***
*** auto-runs a ~5s gyro calibration first. The robot will NOT move    ***
*** right away - keep it completely still and don't touch it for      ***
*** ~5 seconds after that first command. Every command after the      ***
*** first reuses that calibration and does not re-trigger it.         ***

*** IMPORTANT: this plan includes raw MOTOR,B step(s) (see 'STEER,US' ***
*** below). Those have NO self-stopping command yet, and the STM's    ***
*** 1-second heartbeat watchdog will auto-STOP them (and print an     ***
*** unsolicited ERR,HB_TIMEOUT) if more than ~1s passes between your  ***
*** commands - poll ENC,GET,CM quickly and repeatedly. If it auto-    ***
*** stops early, that's the watchdog working as intended, not a bug - ***
*** just check the encoder reading and re-send MOTOR,B to continue.   ***

--- leg: S -> obstacle 0 ---
[1] FORWARD RIGHT 30cm, 57 deg
    > TURN,START,R=300,A=57
    > TURN,STATUS      <- repeat until state=DONE, then continue
[2] FORWARD STRAIGHT 50cm
    > STRAIGHT,GOCM,50
    > STRAIGHT,STATUS   <- repeat until state=DONE, then continue
[3] FORWARD RIGHT 30cm, 57 deg
    > TURN,START,R=300,A=57
    > TURN,STATUS      <- repeat until state=DONE, then continue
[4] FORWARD STRAIGHT 40cm
    > STRAIGHT,GOCM,40
    > STRAIGHT,STATUS   <- repeat until state=DONE, then continue
[5] FORWARD LEFT 10cm, 19 deg
    > TURN,START,R=300,A=-19
    > TURN,STATUS      <- repeat until state=DONE, then continue

--- leg: 0 -> obstacle 1 ---
[6] FORWARD STRAIGHT 10cm
    > STRAIGHT,GOCM,10
    > STRAIGHT,STATUS   <- repeat until state=DONE, then continue
[7] REVERSE RIGHT 50cm, 95 deg
    > STEER,US,1912
    > ENC,RESET
    > MOTOR,B,-2900,-2900
    > ENC,GET,CM       <- repeat FAST (under 1s between polls - see heartbeat warning below); STOP once (l_cm+r_cm)/2 reaches 50cm (going backward, values will read negative)
    > STOP
    ** at this point the robot should be at obstacle 1's viewing pose - take the photo now **
